# Data sources
- `daily_policy_bond.csv`: Bank of Canada series BD.CDN.5YR.DQ.YLD and V39079 as compiled in John Khan's repository `ALENZACODES/canada-policy-rate-bond-yield-mortgage-rate`, file `data/daily_policy_bond.csv` at commit 815cb7b, copied unchanged (MIT licence). SHA-256: `40311d546c79eb75a165898f4dba862b3f71c6c619b2def680abaa4528aab162`, the same as the file at that commit, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ALENZACODES/canada-policy-rate-bond-yield-mortgage-rate/815cb7b/data/daily_policy_bond.csv (checked 2026-10-19; `code/ch01/test_goc_case.py` repeats the check when a network is available). The file runs to 2026-10-15; the Chapter 1 and Chapter 3 code cut it at 2026-09-30.
- `goc_bills_bonds.csv`: Bank of Canada Valet API, series TB.CDN.90D.MID, TB.CDN.180D.MID, TB.CDN.1Y.MID, BD.CDN.2YR.DQ.YLD, BD.CDN.3YR.DQ.YLD, BD.CDN.5YR.DQ.YLD, BD.CDN.7YR.DQ.YLD, BD.CDN.10YR.DQ.YLD, BD.CDN.LONG.DQ.YLD, 2001-01-01 to 2026-09-30, accessed 2026-10-19.
- `usdcad_rates.csv`: built by `scripts/build_usdcad.py` from the raw downloads in `raw/`. USD/CAD is the Bank of Canada noon rate IEXE0101 (legacy series, Valet API) to 2016-12-30 and the daily average FXUSDCAD (Valet API) from 2017-01-03; the Canadian 1-month Treasury bill yield is TB.CDN.30D.MID (Valet API); the US 1-month yield is FRED series DGS1MO. Dates 2007-05-01 to 2026-09-29 (the dates with a USD/CAD rate), accessed 2026-10-19; the raw downloads are cut at 2026-09-30. Yields are in percent; a missing yield is left empty.
- `ff_factors_monthly.csv` and `industry10_monthly.csv` (not included in the public copy; build them yourself): run `python scripts/build_french.py --download` from the repository root, which saves the CSV files from the library's zip archives at https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html into `raw/french/` and builds both files, keeping months up to August 2026. The library revises past months from time to time, so a later download can differ slightly from the release used in the book. Built from the CSV files in `raw/french/` (Kenneth R. French Data Library: Fama/French 5 factors 2x3, Fama/French 3 factors, momentum factor and 10 industry portfolios, release built from CRSP 202608), July 1963 to August 2026, accessed 2026-10-19. Returns are monthly, in percent; the industry file holds value-weighted returns, equal-weighted returns (`ew_` columns) and market capitalisation as number of firms times average firm size (`cap_` columns).
- `ff_daily.csv` (not included in the public copy; `python scripts/build_french.py --download` builds it with the two monthly files): daily market excess return and one month bill rate from the Fama/French 3 factors daily file of the Kenneth R. French Data Library (release built from CRSP 202608), with `mkt = mkt_rf + rf`, 1963-07-01 to 2026-08-31, 15,897 trading days, accessed 2026-10-19. Returns are in percent per day, to two decimals as published.
- `fred_markets_daily.csv`: FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis: DGS10, DCOILWTICO, DEXUSEU, DEXJPUS, DEXCAUS (market yield on 10 year Treasury securities, WTI crude oil spot price from the US Energy Information Administration, and the Federal Reserve H.10 noon buying rates for EUR, JPY and CAD), daily, 1999-01-04 to 2026-09-30, accessed 2026-10-19. Built by `scripts/pull_fred.py`, which also saves each series, cut at the end date, in `raw/`.
- `crypto_daily.csv`: (not included in the public copy; `python scripts/pull_fred.py` rebuilds it) FRED series CBBTCUSD, CBETHUSD, Coinbase bitcoin and ether prices in US dollars, daily, 2014-12-01 to 2026-09-30, accessed 2026-10-19.
- `real_assets_quarterly.csv`: FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, public domain series: BOGZ1FL075035503Q (Financial Accounts of the United States, Z.1, commercial real estate price index; from 1996 the Federal Reserve chains the CoStar U.S. Composite Index excluding multifamily, value weighted, and before 1996 it averages three appraisal-based price series), BOGZ1FL123164105Q and BOGZ1FR123164105Q (Z.1, equity REITs, corporate equities, liability, level and revaluation) and USSTHPI (US Federal Housing Finance Agency, all-transactions house price index), quarterly, 1970-01-01 to 2026-04-01, accessed 2026-10-19. Built by `scripts/pull_ch10.py`, which also saves each series, cut at the end date, in `raw/`.
- `real_assets_monthly.csv`: FRED, public domain series: CPIAUCSL (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, CPI for all urban consumers, seasonally adjusted), TB3MS and GS10 (Federal Reserve H.15, 3 month Treasury bill secondary market rate and 10 year constant maturity yield, monthly averages), WPU01, WPU0851 and WPU0811 (BLS producer price indexes for farm products, for logs, bolts, timber, pulpwood and wood chips, and for softwood lumber), MCOILWTICO and MHHNGSP (US Energy Information Administration, WTI and Henry Hub spot prices, monthly averages), 1970-01-01 to 2026-09-01, accessed 2026-10-19. Built by `scripts/pull_ch10.py`.
- `eia_curves_monthly.csv`: US Energy Information Administration, public domain: NYMEX crude oil (Cushing, OK) and natural gas (Henry Hub) futures, contracts 1 to 4, monthly averages of daily closing prices, which the EIA published until April 2024; US ending stocks of crude oil excluding the Strategic Petroleum Reserve (thousand barrels) and US working gas in underground storage (million cubic feet), monthly, from the EIA history pages at https://www.eia.gov/dnav/, 1973-12-01 to 2026-07-01, accessed 2026-10-19. Built by `scripts/pull_ch10.py`.
- `calpers_pe_funds.csv`: CalPERS, Private Equity Program Fund Performance Review, all active partnerships as of March 31, 2026, https://www.calpers.ca.gov/investments/about-investment-office/investment-organization/pep-fund-performance-print, accessed 2026-10-19. Not redistributed; built by `scripts/pull_ch11.py`.
- `damodaran_industry.csv`, `damodaran_erp.csv`: Aswath Damodaran, data sets at https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/ (US industry averages, January 2026 update: vebitda, wacc, fundgrEB; historical implied equity risk premiums, 1960 to 2025: histimpl), accessed 2026-10-19. Not redistributed; built by `scripts/pull_ch11.py`.
- `goyal_welch_monthly.csv`: Amit Goyal and Ivo Welch, equity premium prediction data, monthly sheet of the update to December 2025 (Google Drive file 1qwpl2R_DNujpU5YUkk8lacP1tTeMb9iJ, linked from https://sites.google.com/view/agoyal145 as "Updated data (up to 2025)"), accessed 2026-10-19, January 1926 to December 2025. Not redistributed; built by `scripts/pull_ch12.py`.
- `ofr_hedge_funds_quarterly.csv`: US Office of Financial Research, Hedge Fund Monitor, Form PF Statistics (https://www.financialresearch.gov/hedge-fund-monitor/datasets/fpf/), aggregated SEC Form PF responses of Qualifying Hedge Funds through the OFR API (https://data.financialresearch.gov/hf/v1/series/full): gross and net quarterly returns and leverage by strategy (net asset-weighted means), investor and portfolio liquidity, gates, gross and net assets and fund counts, quarterly, 2013-03-31 to 2026-06-30, accessed 2026-10-22. A US government work, reproduced with credit as https://www.financialresearch.gov/legal-notices/ requests. Built by `scripts/pull_hedge.py`, which also saves the API responses in `raw/`.
- `fung_hsieh_ptfs.csv`: (not included in the public copy; `python scripts/pull_hedge.py` rebuilds it) David A. Hsieh's data library (https://people.duke.edu/~dah7/HFRFData.htm), the trend-following risk factors of Fung and Hsieh (2001), file https://people.duke.edu/~dah7/DataLibrary/TF-Fac.xls, monthly returns of the PTFS bond, currency, commodity, short-term interest rate and stock index lookback straddles, 1994-01 to 2026-08, accessed 2026-10-22. Made available for academic research with citation of Fung and Hsieh (2001) and of the file's URL; not redistributed.
- `bdc_quarterly.csv`: US Securities and Exchange Commission, EDGAR XBRL company facts (https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/), facts from the 10-Q and 10-K filings of nineteen listed business development companies (ARCC, BXSL, MAIN, OBDC, GBDC, HTGC, FSK, TSLX, CSWC, OCSL, GSBD, BBDC, FDUS, CGBD, PFLT, NMFC, SLRC, TCPC, GAIN): net asset value per share, investments at fair value and cost, assets, liabilities, net assets, investment income, net investment income, the result of operations and fees, quarterly to 2026-06-30, accessed 2026-10-22. Information on sec.gov is public and may be copied or further distributed (https://www.sec.gov/privacy). Built by `scripts/pull_bdc.py`, which saves the facts it uses in `raw/sec_bdc_facts.json`.
